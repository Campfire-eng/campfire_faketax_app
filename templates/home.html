Where things are

Buttons and panelsLive inside Campfire on invoice pages: Calculate tax, Tax breakdown, View in FakeTax, and the FakeTax tab. They only show while the workspace is connected.
EventsInvoice and credit memo changes arrive at /events; with auto-calculate on, FakeTax writes tax a moment later.
